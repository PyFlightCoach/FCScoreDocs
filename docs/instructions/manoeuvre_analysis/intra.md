# Intra Downgrades

Intra downgrades measure how well each element was flown on its own. See [Intra scoring](../../process/intra.md) for how they are calculated and the [downgrade reference](../../downgrades/index.md) for each one.

In the manoeuvre view click **intra** to see the table. Downgrades are grouped; click a group heading to expand it and see the individual downgrades and the points lost for each. Click a downgrade to show where it applies in the 3D view, along with its description and judging guide text at the bottom of the screen.

The group total is the sum of the points lost for its downgrades. The **Total** row is the intra score loss for the manoeuvre.
