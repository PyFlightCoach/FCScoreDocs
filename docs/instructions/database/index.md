# Flight Database

The flight database holds flights that pilots have chosen to share, with their scores. It drives the leaderboards and the map. Competitions also draw their results from it.

## Getting flights in

You need an account with a verified email address ([User Management](../users/index.md)). Score the flight as normal, wait for the analysis to finish, then use **Upload** on the results page. You choose a privacy level (basic, view flown or view analysis) and may add a comment. See [Results](../scoring/results.md#uploading). Only one sequence per recording can currently be shared. Flights can also be linked to competition rounds; see [Competitions](../competitions/index.md).

## Leaderboards

**Leaderboard** in the database menu (shown to users with a verified email) opens the leaderboards page. Choose a discipline and a schedule, then:

- **Leaderboard** ranks pilots by their best score; **History** lists flights as they were uploaded.
- Order flights by **Date flown** or **Date uploaded**.
- Settings such as the analysis **Version**, **Difficulty** (Hard, Moderate, Easy), a manoeuvre number, a date range, and how many days or flights to include can be changed, then click **Apply**. **Reset to defaults** restores the standard view.

The table shows pilot, country, date and score, and the settings include options to add your own best or latest flight. Click a row to show details of that flight. Pilot names link to the pilot's [user page](../users/index.md#user-pages).

## Map

The **Map** page plots the position of the flights in the current leaderboard selection. Nearby flights are grouped into one marker; its size shows the number of flights and its colour and label the highest score. Hover for details. Change the leaderboard selection to change what is plotted.
