# Results

The results page opens from **Results** in the Flight menu, or automatically after segmentation. The **Sequence Results** panel is on the left; the flight is shown in 3D with each manoeuvre coloured.

## The table

Each row is a manoeuvre: its number, name, K factor and score. Scores are out of 10 and the large figure at the bottom is the sum of score multiplied by K, with the percentage of the available points beneath it. While a manoeuvre is being analysed its stage is shown; queued manoeuvres show an hourglass and failed ones show **Failed**. Click a row to open its [detail view](../manoeuvre_analysis/index.md).

## Buttons

- **Info** shows flight details (source, pilot, schedule, boot time, total score, wind estimate), a **Copy link to this flight** button for database flights, and analysis settings.
- **Upload** shares the flight to the database; see below.
- **Analysis** holds the scoring settings: **Analysis version**, **Difficulty** (Hard, Moderate, Easy), **Truncate downgrades**, and **Run all analyses**.
- For AcroWRX flights, **Adjust box** and **Edit splits** return to the box and manoeuvre pages.

A **Compare Flight** panel lets you pick another database flight (with filters such as **Only mine**) to show its trace and stored scores in an extra **Comp.** column.

If the recording holds several sequences or repeats, a row of sequence buttons and a repetition list appear above these buttons: see [Scoring against several schedules](multiple_schedules.md).

## Uploading

**Upload** opens "Share to the database". You must be logged in with a verified email, and the analysis must be complete. Choose **Privacy** (basic, view flown, view analysis), add **Comments** and click **Upload**. If the button is not available, the window says why (for example "Not logged in" or "Email not verified"). Administrators, and some AcroWRX administrators, can upload an AcroWRX flight for another pilot. After uploading you see where the flight places on the leaderboard.

See also [Flight Database](../database/index.md).
